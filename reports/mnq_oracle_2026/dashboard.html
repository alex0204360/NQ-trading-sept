The answer so far

Development rejected trading in favor of abstaining. A forest challenger made a small base-cost final-period gain, but lost under higher costs and was not the preselected policy. It cannot be promoted after inspecting this result.

Data and limitations

28,312 source bars → 28,311 retained; 866 matched oracle entries → 813 feature-ready examples. Nine missing teacher timestamps, 75 gaps, 26 zero-volume bars and one partial candle are audited. Original NQ research and its sealed 2025 holdout are unchanged.

  • Only roughly two weeks of exported oracle labels; final segment is approximately two sessions including partial Monday.
  • Timestamp convention, TradingView versus Yahoo feed alignment, and continuous-contract roll mapping lack independent verification.
  • ALMA offset/sigma/UTC anchor are prespecified assumptions, not verified exact proprietary Pine settings.
  • Oracle labels are future-informed supervision. Same-row features are available one minute after the assumed teacher timestamp; results are delayed imitation, not foresight.
  • Negative labels assume the exported teacher trade coverage is complete; missing teacher bars and feature warm-up remove events.
  • Bar-close drawdown misses intrabar excursions; OHLC cannot certify fills, queue priority or bid/ask spreads. Cost scenarios are assumptions, not broker quotes.
  • Gap liquidations at the next observed open are explicitly unresolved exposure, not reconstructed tradable paths.
  • No independent market-source verification or live deployment approval; original NQ 2025 remains unopened.

Smallest next experiment

First verify the teacher's exact non-lookahead settings, interval timing and contract mapping against a licensed contract-specific feed. Then collect a new, untouched period and preregister the same frozen students for forward paper evaluation. Do not select the forest retrospectively or keep tuning this final sample.
